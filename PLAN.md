# JobHunt — Smart Job Aggregator App

**Features**

- **Profile setup** with two options: manually enter your job title, skills, location, and experience — or paste your resume text to auto-extract those fields
- **Job feed** showing listings from all major boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, Remote.co, AngelList) with the source clearly labeled
- **Smart filters** to narrow results by salary range, location, remote/hybrid/on-site, experience level, and job type (full-time, contract, etc.)
- **Save/bookmark jobs** you're interested in and view them in a dedicated saved list
- **Job detail view** with full description, requirements, salary info, and a direct link to apply on the original posting
- **Profile screen** to view and edit your parsed/entered profile info at any time

**Design**

- Dark mode with deep navy/charcoal backgrounds and vibrant teal accent color — inspired by Coinbase and modern fintech apps
- Job cards with subtle glassmorphism effect, source badges with distinct colors per platform
- Smooth micro-interactions on bookmark tap and filter toggles
- Clean typography with good hierarchy — large bold titles, muted secondary text
- Bottom sheet for filters with pill-style toggles and sliders
- Empty states with helpful illustrations when no results or no saved jobs

**Screens**

- **Jobs tab (Home)**: Scrollable feed of job cards with a search bar at top and filter button; pull-to-refresh for new results
- **Saved tab**: List of all bookmarked jobs, swipe to remove
- **Profile tab**: Your profile info (title, skills, location, experience) with edit capability and resume paste option
- **Job Detail page**: Opens as a stack screen over tabs — full job description, company info, salary, and "Apply" button linking to the original posting
- **Filter sheet**: Bottom modal with salary range, location, work type, experience level filters

**App Icon**

- Dark navy background with a stylized magnifying glass over a briefcase silhouette in teal/cyan — clean and modern
